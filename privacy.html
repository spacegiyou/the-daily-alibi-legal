The Daily Alibi — Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Operator and privacy contact: Geunil You (유근일), an individual creator using the name GiYou in the Republic of Korea.
spacegiyou@gmail.com

This Notice explains the App-specific data practices of the first-release, prewritten mystery pilot. Reddit separately operates Reddit accounts, authentication, posts, comments, and the hosting platform. Please also read the Terms of Use.

1. Data and purposes

Reddit username and account availability
Reddit supplies your signed-in username so the App can save one player’s choices, prevent duplicate participation, retrieve only your own private result, and process deletion. The App checks the availability of participating accounts for limited cleanup; it does not use that check to profile you.
Question-card choices and private verdict
The App stores your selected card identifiers, private suspect choice, and whether the verdict’s last edit was early. These data operate editable ballots, select the community’s ten questions, enforce deadlines, and calculate your private current-case score. The case supports at most 1,000 participating accounts.
Case and reliability records
Case/post identifiers, phase and expiry times, vote totals, selected-card identifiers, and small operation/retry records are used to show the right case, avoid duplicate posts, reconcile delayed updates, and complete deletion. The long-lived case index contains no usernames, ballots, or verdicts.
Participation confirmation
Before saving a vote or verdict, you must affirm eligibility, agree to the Terms, and acknowledge this Notice using an initially unchecked control. Your confirmation is sent with the save request for validation. Its checked state stays only in the open page’s memory, and no separate confirmation history, birth date, or identity document is stored. Reading and deletion do not require checking it.
Voluntary support correspondence
If you email support, the operator receives your email address, message, and any details you choose to include, solely to handle the request, verify its connection to an account when necessary, and keep required records. This correspondence is separate from gameplay storage. Do not send passwords, government identification, payment details, or other sensitive information.

The game has no free-form question input. It does not ask for a real name, email address, password, payment information, device or advertising identifier, or location through gameplay. It does not ingest new Reddit comments for gameplay, sell personal data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Saving votes and verdicts is optional, but the limited account-linked processing above is necessary to save those choices. You can decline participation and read available case material instead. Participation confirmation does not authorize advertising, external AI processing, or any unrelated use. We obtain any additional consent required by applicable law and handle requests and legal obligations as required by that law.

2. What other players can see

Players can see the fictional case, question cards, shared vote totals, selected answers as they unlock, the records update, and the final solution. They cannot retrieve your individual votes, verdict, or score through the App. Your own result is shown privately at reveal, not published to a leaderboard.

Comments that you independently write on Reddit have the visibility set by Reddit and the community. The App does not submit a comment or score for you. “Private” here means not exposed to other players by the App; authorized platform or operator access needed to run, secure, or support the service remains possible.

3. Hosting and separate services

  • Reddit / Devvit: gameplay runs on Reddit’s platform, and App records are stored in Devvit Redis. Reddit handles its own accounts, technical logs, cookies, and service data under the Reddit Privacy Policy.
  • No external AI or database: this release has no HTTP permission, external AI calls, or external database. It does not send gameplay data to OpenAI or another AI provider. All case dialogue is prewritten.
  • GitHub Pages: these public policy documents are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages contain no forms, analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, external fonts, or application cookies. GitHub may process network and request information, such as IP address and browser/request details, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The App does not attach usernames, votes, or verdicts to policy links.
  • Gmail: voluntary email to spacegiyou@gmail.com is received through Google’s Gmail service. Google’s processing is described in the Google Privacy Policy. No gameplay email is sent automatically.

These services may process information in countries other than your own under their respective policies. The App’s storage deadline below does not set those services’ independent log, account, backup, or legal-retention periods. The operator does not promise deletion of records controlled independently by Reddit, GitHub, or Google. Data may be disclosed when a valid legal obligation requires it.

4. Retention and deletion

New pilot gameplay: the case-linked username, card choices, verdict, and score inputs expire no later than 30 days after the case starts. Reading or changing them does not extend that fixed deadline. There is no new permanent cumulative score history. The non-personal case index may remain for operation and duplicate-post prevention; expired case references are pruned.

Earlier private previews: where genuine pre-pilot records already exist, old question/comment copies, verdicts, and case-linked records use their previous rolling 30-day expiry; weekly/index records may remain for up to 60 days. Historical cumulative scores may remain until a user wipe. Pending deletion records can remain until cleanup completes. Those historical records are not imported into the new game, and fresh pilot installations do not create them. A full user-deletion request covers retained preview data too.

Support email: the operator’s policy is to delete resolved correspondence from the support mailbox within 90 days after resolution, unless a specific legal preservation requirement applies. This is an operator-managed mailbox practice, not an automatic in-game deletion function. A required retained record is restricted to that purpose and removed when the requirement ends.

The App processes a deletion request through the following options:

  1. While signed in, open How to play → Request data deletion → Confirm deletion request.
  2. Ask a community moderator to use the App’s Wipe user data menu for your account.
  3. Email spacegiyou@gmail.com for deletion or assistance.

The App removes current participation and recalculates associated totals as appropriate. Historical cleanup, including removal of old app-authored replies, may need queued retries. An accepted request does not mean every historical Reddit reply has already disappeared. A deletion request does not delete your Reddit account or native Reddit comments. Those are managed through Reddit. If you choose to participate again after deletion, new choices can be stored until the case’s original expiry.

Deleting a case post clears its associated new participation and closes that board. The scheduler checks one participating account’s availability per tick; a confirmed unavailable result removes that account’s new pilot participation. This can include suspended accounts. Lookup errors preserve data for retry. This is not an instant account-deletion notification, so please use the deletion options above if you need direct assistance. The fixed 30-day limit remains an additional safeguard.

5. Your requests and choices

You may contact the operator to request access, correction, or deletion of App records. Other rights, including restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of required consent, or a complaint to a competent data-protection authority, apply where provided by applicable law. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of prior processing; it may prevent the App from continuing to save your choices.

We may ask for a minimal account-verification step before changing private records, preferably through your signed-in Reddit account. Do not send a password or identity document. We will respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any lawful reason a request cannot be fulfilled. Contact support if an in-game request fails.

6. Age, security, and updates

The pilot is for users aged 14 or older who also meet any higher Reddit or applicable local minimum age. It is not directed to children below that age. If you believe such a child’s data has been saved, contact the operator so it can be addressed.

The App authenticates changes through Reddit, isolates each player’s private choices, limits case retention, and keeps unrevealed case information server-side. No system can guarantee perfect security. Report suspected exposure or misuse to spacegiyou@gmail.com; the operator will investigate and provide notices required by applicable law and platform rules.

Material changes to these practices will be published with an updated effective date, with additional notice or consent where required. A future AI mode is not part of this Notice’s first-release processing; it would require a separate review and updated disclosure before activation.
